Don’t forget your tissues when you head to the theaters to see Academy Award winner Chloé Zhao’s new film. Based on Maggie O’Farrell’s novel of the same name, Hamnet presents the love story and tragedy that inspired William Shakespeare’s “Hamlet.” Paul Mescal and Jessie Buckely star as the playwright and his bride through every stage of life from blissful companions to broken mourners and beyond as they begin to heal through art. — Adrienne
